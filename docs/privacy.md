# Polyscan Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 30 October 2026*

Polyscan is a document scanner that works on your phone, without an account and without a cloud.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Polyscan has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## Your scans

- Photos, scans, recognized text and exported files are stored only on your device.
- Text recognition (OCR) runs on your device. Your pages are never uploaded.
- Files leave your device only when you share them yourself, for example through the share sheet.
- Deleting the app deletes all its documents.

## Camera and photos

- The camera is used only to scan documents when you tap Scan.
- Photo and file access is used only for the pictures and files you pick to import.

## Internet use

Polyscan connects to the internet only to download the text-recognition language files you choose. They are downloaded from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`). No scans, text or personal data are sent. GitHub, like any web server, may see your IP address when a file is downloaded; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Purchases

The one-time unlock is bought through Apple's App Store (or Google Play on Android). The store handles the payment; Polyscan never sees your payment details. The app only learns whether the unlock was bought.

## Children

Polyscan collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: see the [Polyscan support page](support.md).
