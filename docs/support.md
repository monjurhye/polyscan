# Polyscan Support

Polyscan scans documents, reads the text in 100+ languages on your phone, and makes searchable PDFs. No account, no subscription, no ads.

## Get help

Report a problem or ask a question: **[open an issue](https://github.com/monjurhye/polyscan/issues/new)**. Please include your phone model, the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and, if you can, the language of the document.

## Common questions

**How many scans are free?**
The first 10 scans are free. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited scans. There is no subscription.

**I bought the unlock on another phone. How do I get it back?**
Open Settings › Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID (or Google account).

**How do I add a language?**
Settings › Text languages, then download the language. After that it works offline.

**Why is some text wrong?**
- Use good, even light and hold the phone flat over the page.
- Pick the right language before reading the text.
- Handwriting is read poorly; printed text works best.

**Where are my files stored?**
Only on your phone. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

**Which formats can I share?**
Searchable PDF, Word (.docx), plain text and images (.jpg).
