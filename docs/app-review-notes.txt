Polyscan is a free document scanner with ads (Google AdMob, Google UMP consent, App Tracking Transparency). It scans with the VisionKit document camera or imports photos/files, reads the text on the device (Apple Vision / Tesseract, 100+ languages) and shares searchable PDF, Word, text or images. No account, no login, no user-generated content; scans never leave the phone. One non-consumable in-app purchase, "Remove ads" ($9.99), removes all ads: Settings › Remove ads, and Settings › Restore purchase.

How to test: tap Scan to scan a page, or the photo button to import one; open the document, tap Text › Recognize text, then Share.
